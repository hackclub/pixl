# Upvotes and collectibles

^ Upvotes are how the community says a build is cool. You can't buy them and they don't convert into pixels or cash.

## Voting

Browse the community feed and upvote what you like. One vote per player per project, and votes are permanent. You can't upvote your own projects.

## Collectibles

Your upvote score is the total number of upvotes your projects have received. You spend it on **collectibles**: in-game cosmetics and badges for your character that pixels can't buy.

Since votes never expire, your balance keeps creeping up as people find your older projects.

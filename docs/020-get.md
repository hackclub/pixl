# What do you actually get

Every hour of work you ship earns you *pixels*. The rate starts at {{basePx}}/hr on your first build and climbs up to {{maxPx}}/hr as your project accumulates Restoration Energy (check [Restoration Energy and levels](/docs/energy/) for the exact math).

Trials come with custom rewards tailored to the build: things like custom sticker sheets, Steam grants, Flipper Zeros, Robux, or PCB manufacturing credits. When your trial ship gets approved, you pick one of two options:
1. **Take the trial prize** (plus pixels for any hours you tracked past the trial's minimum required time).
2. **Take all your hours as raw pixels** and pass on the special prize.

If you don't care about a specific trial bounty, just take the pixels and buy whatever you actually want from the shop. Either way, finishing a trial drops {{trialBonusRe}} bonus Restoration Energy on top, boosting your level and the Core Vault faster than a standard invention.

Pixels go straight into your in-game balance. Spend them on everything from meme stickers and signed organizer photos up to real hardware like a MacBook Air.

If you hit a wall, ask in `#pixl` on the Hack Club Slack. There's a dedicated help channel and bots tuned to the game mechanics. Someone in `#pixl` has definitely run into the exact problem you're dealing with, just drop a message and ask.

# What do you actually get

Every hour you ship gives you *pixels*. It starts at {{basePx}} an hour and increase as you log more hours in your project, up to {{maxPx}} an hour. [Restoration Energy and levels](/docs/energy/) explains this with more details. Every trial gives you a special reward, specific to this trial that are things like a custom sticker sheet, a Steam grant, a Flipper Zero, robux, PCB manufacturing budget, always in theme with what the quest is. When a trial ship is approved, you need to choose in between taking the prize (+ pixels at your normal rate if you shipped more hours than the minimum hour requirement for this trial), or taking the pixels that this project earned in his globality, without the special reward. So if the prize isn't the one you want, take the pixels and buy what you actually need from the shop. Either way the trial ship gives {{trialBonusRe}} bonus Restoration Energy on top, so trials always move your level and the vault further than the same hours built in a general invention.

Pixels themselves go into the in game balance, where you can spend them on cool and funny item like an org signed photo all the way up to bigger stuff like a MacBook Air.

If you get stuck at any point, feel free to ask in the pixl channels on Slack, we have a help channel and 2 bots that know the subject and that could help you. Someone in #pixl has almost definitely run into the same wall you're stuck on. If you really can't find an answer, just ask.

Shipping

Building the thing is only half of it, you also have to ship it. Shipping just means:

  • finish building your project
  • write up what you made, what it does, and what you learned
  • submit it for review
  • get approved
  • pixels and rewards land in your account

Before you hit submit, look at what a good submission actually looks like. Reviewers care about seeing the real project, a clear explanation of what you built, and something that shows it's actually yours and not a copy.

Ship requirements lists everything the submit button actually checks: repo, demo, thumbnail, AI notes, journal length, the hours cutoff. Worth a read before your first one.
